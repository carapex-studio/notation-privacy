Notation privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · first effective 23 November 2026 · applies to Notation 0.9 (beta) for Android

In short: your notebooks stay on your tablet. Notation has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Only two things go online, and only when you use them: Google's on-device translation, when you choose to translate, and picture searches you make, which go to the free picture library you search.

Notation is a notes app for writing by hand and reading PDFs on Android tablets. It is published by Carapex (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What Notation stores, and where

Your notebooks, pages, handwriting, text, images and PDFs you add, folders, tags, the Trash and your settings are stored on your tablet, in Notation’s private storage. We have no server and never receive them.

Translation while reading

If you ask Notation to translate a passage, it uses Google’s ML Kit:

  • The first time you translate into a language, Notation downloads that language’s translation model from Google. You can remove models in Settings.
  • The translation itself happens on your tablet. The text you translate is never sent anywhere.
  • ML Kit sends Google limited technical information to keep its models working and to measure their performance: the device model, maker and Android version, Notation’s version, an identifier for this installation (not tied to you or your account), performance figures, error codes, and which languages were translated or identified. Google sends it encrypted (HTTPS) and does not pass it to third parties. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

If you never translate, no models are downloaded. Looking up a word’s meaning uses a dictionary built into the app and works offline.

Pictures from the web

The Image card can search free pictures online. If you never search there, nothing is sent. When you do:

  • Notation sends the words you search for (and the kind of picture you picked, such as photos or drawings) to Wikimedia Commons, run by the Wikimedia Foundation, and, in versions of Notation that offer it, to Pixabay. The request also says it comes from Notation and which version. Nothing else about you or your notes is sent: no account, no identifier of yours and none of your notebooks. (Pixabay also receives Notation’s own access key, which is the same for everyone.)
  • The small previews you see, and the picture you choose, are fetched from that same service.
  • Like any website, the service receives your device’s internet (IP) address with each request. Its own privacy policy applies to what it receives: Wikimedia’s privacy policy and Pixabay’s privacy policy.
  • Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS). Notation has no server of its own, so we never receive or keep your searches. The app remembers your last few searches and their results only while it is open, to show them again; they are gone when Notation closes.
  • A picture you place is saved in your notebook like any image you add, with its credit (title, author and licence) shown in the Image card.

Backups you choose

  • Back up to a file, and automatic backup: Notation writes a backup file to a file or folder you choose with Android’s file picker (which may be a storage provider you pick). You control that file.
  • Android backup: if your device’s backup is on, Android may save Notation’s small library lists (folder and tag names and the Trash list) and its settings to your Google account, never your notebooks. You control this in Android’s settings.

This version of Notation does not back up to Google Drive or any cloud service of ours.

Sharing, importing and the clipboard

  • When you share or export a notebook or page, it goes only to the app you choose.
  • When you import a PDF or image, Notation reads only the file you pick.
  • Notation reads your clipboard only when you choose Paste.

Feedback

When you send feedback from Settings, Notation opens an email in your own mail app with your message and, if you leave the option on, Notation’s version and your tablet’s model and Android version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use it only to reply and to improve Notation.

Security

Your notebooks are kept in Notation’s private storage on your tablet, which Android keeps apart from other apps. Notation itself sends nothing over the internet except the picture searches you make, which travel encrypted (HTTPS) to the picture service; the translation information described above travels encrypted (HTTPS) to Google. Backup files and exports go only where you put them, so keep them somewhere you trust.

What Notation does not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking of our own, no crash-report service, no selling or sharing of your data, and no cloud sync in this version. Notation does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Children

Notation is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your notebooks stay on your tablet until you delete them. Delete notebooks in the app and empty the Trash, or uninstall Notation to remove everything it stored on the tablet. Backup files you made stay where you put them until you delete them. Because we hold no copy of your notes or your searches, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; for anything else, write to us. What a picture service received from a search is covered by that service’s own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
